Newest first, with a readable `transaction` beside the SEC code. Purchases
and sales are the discretionary trades; awards, option exercises and tax
withholding are compensation mechanics, so `purchases_usd` and `sales_usd`
count only non-derivative purchases and sales.

MSTR is not included yet: its Form 4 lines do not record which share class
they are in, and its directors buy the preferred series near $100 par.
