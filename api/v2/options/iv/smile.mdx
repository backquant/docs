---
title: "Single-expiry smile"
openapi: "GET /options/iv/smile"
description: "One expiry's implied-vol smile from the fitted surface, by strike and log-moneyness, with its ATM, risk reversals, butterflies and arbitrage checks."
---

One expiry's smile, rebuilt from its SVI fit between its lowest and highest
quoted strikes and never past them (`method: svi`), or its row of the surface grid where the smile
did not fit (`method: interp`, mostly same-day expiries). Each point gives
the strike, the log-moneyness against the expiry's own forward and the vol
in percent.

The response adds the expiry's ATM vol, 25- and 10-delta risk reversals and
butterflies, the fit error `smile_rmse`, and its static-arbitrage checks:
`butterfly_free` (non-negative density across the quoted strikes, with
`min_density_g`) and `calendar_free` (its total variance is at or above the
previous fitted expiry's). Both are null on an `interp` row.

`expiry` takes a token (`30OCT26`) or an ISO date (`2026-10-30`); omitted,
the nearest fitted expiry is returned. `available_expiries` lists every
expiry you can ask for, including fitted ones past the grid's 180 days.

`call_ivs` and `put_ivs` are deprecated and always null.
