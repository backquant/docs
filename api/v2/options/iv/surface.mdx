The fitted implied-vol surface out to 180 days. `z_grid[i][j]` is the vol
in percent at tenor `y_grid[i]` (days, ascending) and strike `x_grid[j]`.
Cells are null where the expiry never traded that far out; nothing is
extrapolated to fill them.

`expiry_forwards` gives each row's forward and how it was found, the smile
method and fit error, the raw SVI parameters `svi` where it fitted, its
years to expiry `t`, the log-moneyness span of its quotes `k_range`, its
quoted ATM and wing vols (`market`) and its quote count. `long_expiries`
carries the same for expiries past 180 days, which are fitted but not
drawn on the grid. `delta_surface` is the same surface by delta.

Calendar arbitrage is removed on the grid. How every step works, and what is
checked, is on [Vol surface methodology](/concepts/vol-surface).
