---
title: "Greek profile"
openapi: "GET /options/greeks/{greek}"
---

Net greek exposure per strike. `greek` is one of `delta` (DEX),
`theta`, `vanna`, `charm`, `vega`. Optional `?dte_max` recomputes from
the raw chain limited to short-DTE contracts.

Default **`positioning=flow`**. See [Positioning](/concepts/positioning).
