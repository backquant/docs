title: "Volatility risk premium"
openapi: "GET /options/vrp"
description: "Volatility Risk Premium. Difference between implied and realized vol; positive = options expensive."
---

Difference between implied and realized vol. Positive = options expensive vs realized; sustained `vrp < 0` is usually a buy-vol signal.
