---
title: "Per-expiry summary"
openapi: "GET /options/expiry-summary"
---

One row per active expiry with ATM IV, 25Δ skew, PCR, OI, net GEX, plus
OPEX enrichment (`type`, `is_anchor`, `expiry_date`, `notional_oi_usd`,
`max_pain`).

## OI and notional

| Field | Unit |
|---|---|
| `call_oi` / `put_oi` / `total_oi` | **Coin** of underlying (multi-venue sum) |
| `notional_oi_usd` | `total_oi × spot_price` when spot is present |

See [Venue coverage](/concepts/venues) for which venues feed the blend.
