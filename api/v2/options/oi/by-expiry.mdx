---
title: "OI by expiry"
openapi: "GET /options/oi/by-expiry"
---

Open interest aggregated by expiration with calls and puts split.
`?weekly_only=true` excludes monthly + quarterly anchors.

## Units

| Field | Unit |
|---|---|
| Call / put / total OI | **Coin** of the underlying (BTC, ETH, …) |
| Multi-venue totals | Sum of coin OI across venues on the same normalized instrument |

USD notional ≈ `total_oi × spot`. When present on
[`/v2/options/expiry-summary`](/api/v2/options/expiry-summary),
`notional_oi_usd` is that product.

Filter with `?exchanges=` where supported. Venue list:
[Venue coverage](/concepts/venues).
