Dealer gamma hedge series only: each point is `ΔS% × net_GEX` under the
selected positioning model, plus a cumulative sum of those increments.

Positive ≈ dealer buying pressure from gamma hedging on the move.
Negative ≈ selling.

Default `positioning=flow`. See [Positioning](/concepts/positioning).
