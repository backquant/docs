---
title: "Stress history (Postgres)"
openapi: "GET /gex/stress-history"
---

Long-window record of HVL / call wall / put wall / net GEX from the
persistent store. Suitable for backtests or regime work. Bounded to 30 days.

**Positioning is std only** (same as GEX history). Live REST levels
default to flow.
