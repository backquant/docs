---
title: "Liquidations"
openapi: "GET /market/liquidations"
description: "Long and short liquidations over time, on the same interval grid as the other market series."
---

Long and short liquidations over time, on the same interval grid as
[`/market/candles`](/api/v2/market/candles), so they align with price, OI and
CVD without resampling.

This is the **time series**. For the strike-style views see
[`/liquidation/heatmap`](/api/v2/liquidation/heatmap) and
[`/liquidation/distribution`](/api/v2/liquidation/distribution).

<Tip>
Pulling liquidations alongside price and CVD? One call to
[`/market/bundle`](/api/v2/market/bundle) returns all six series aligned.
</Tip>
